# Parallel Work

Parallel work is the practice of splitting a large task into smaller, independent pieces that can be executed at the same time. When work is truly parallel — meaning no piece depends on the output of another — a team of workers can complete the whole far faster than any single worker could. The key challenge is identifying the boundaries between pieces: each worker must know exactly what to build, where their scope ends, and what they may touch. When those boundaries are clear, workers rarely collide. When they are vague, effort is wasted on coordination, merge conflicts, and arguments about who was right. A well-defined spec turns parallel work from chaos into a straightforward pipeline.
